📖 Yeh Checklist Kyun Zaroori Hai?

Bihar mein zameen se juda malikana haq sirf ek document se nahi, balki kai dastavezon ki ek chain se sabit hota hai — purane Khatiyan se lekar current Jamabandi tak, registry se lekar mutation certificate tak. Bank loan, zameen bechte waqt, ya kisi vivad mein — jab bhi zaroorat padegi, aapko ek saath sabhi dastavez dikhane padte hain.

Zyadatar log yeh sab dastavez alag-alag jagah rakhte hain aur zaroorat padne par pata chalta hai ki koi ek document missing hai. Yeh checklist aapko har zaroori document ka status ek jagah track karne mein madad karti hai, aur missing document ke liye seedhe sahi guide tak pahuncha deti hai.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Sabse zaroori dastavez hain: Khatiyan, Jamabandi/RoR, Registry Deed (Bainama), Dakhil-Kharij Mutation Certificate, LPC, aur chalu varsh ka Bhu-Lagan raseed. Inheritance wali zameen ke liye Vanshavali bhi zaroori hai.
Aapki checklist sirf aapke apne browser mein (localStorage) save hoti hai — yeh data kabhi bhi BighaWala ke server par nahi bheja jaata. Browser cache clear karne par checklist reset ho jaayegi.
Har missing dastavez ke saamne ek "Guide →" link diya gaya hai jo seedhe us dastavez ko banwane/nikalne ki poori step-by-step guide par le jaata hai।
Kuch dastavez (jaise Vanshavali ya Survey Prapatra-20/21) sirf specific situations mein zaroori hote hain — checklist mein aise items "Situation-specific" category mein alag dikhaye gaye hain।
