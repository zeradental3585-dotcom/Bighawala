💡 Tax Bachane Ke Options (Capital Gains Exemptions)

Income Tax Act 2025 (1 April 2026 se lagu) mein purane Section 54B/54EC/54F ab naye numbers se jaane jaate hain — dono niche diye hain.

Section 54B (ab Section 83)

Agricultural Land → Agricultural Land

Agar becha gaya asset agricultural land tha (jo aap/aapke parents ne kam se kam 2 saal use kiya ho), aur aap 2 saal ke andar doosri agricultural land khareedte hain, toh poora gain exempt ho sakta hai.

Section 54EC (ab Section 85)

Capital Gains Bonds (REC/PFC/IRFC)

Gain ka max ₹50 lakh, sale ke 6 mahine ke andar REC/PFC/IRFC bonds mein invest karein — woh amount exempt ho jata hai. 5 saal lock-in, interest (~5.25%) alag se taxable hai.

Section 54F (ab Section 86)

Zameen → Residential House

Poori sale proceeds (sirf gain nahi) ek residential house mein invest karein — pura exempt. Kam invest karne par proportionate exemption milta hai. Cap ₹10 crore.

❓ FAQ — Capital Gains Tax Par Sawaal Jawaab

Q: Kya rural agricultural land bechne par bhi capital gains tax lagta hai?

▼

A: Nahi. Section 2(14)(iii) ke anusar rural agricultural land ek capital asset hi nahi mana jata, isliye iski bikri par koi capital gains tax nahi lagta, TDS nahi katta, aur ITR mein report karne ki zaroorat bhi nahi hoti. Zameen rural tab maani jaati hai jab nearest municipality ki population 10,000 se kam ho, ya population ke hisab se 2/6/8 km se zyada door ho (aerial distance).

Q: Long-term aur short-term capital gains mein kya fark hai?

▼

A: 24 mahine se zyada hold karne par LTCG (Long-Term), 24 mahine ya kam mein STCG (Short-Term). STCG aapki total income mein add hokar slab rate se tax hota hai; LTCG par flat 12.5% (ya eligible ho toh 20% with indexation) lagta hai.

Q: LTCG par kitna tax lagta hai 2026 mein?

▼

A: Standard rate 12.5% (bina indexation). Agar zameen/building 23 July 2024 se pehle khareedi gayi thi, toh 20% with indexation ka option bhi milta hai — jo kam ho wahi choose karein. Dono par 4% cess alag se.

Q: Virasat mein mili zameen ka cost of acquisition kaise nikale?

▼

A: Cost of acquisition = pichle malik (jaise dada/pardada) ne jitne mein khareedi thi. Holding period bhi unke khareedne ke din se hi ginaa jata hai — mutation/registry ki current date se nahi.

Q: Zameen bechne ke baad tax bachane ke kya options hain?

▼

A: Section 54B (agri-to-agri land, 2 saal), Section 54EC (₹50L tak capital gains bonds, 6 mahine), Section 54F (poori proceeds residential house mein). Naye Income Tax Act 2025 mein yeh Section 83, 85 aur 86 hain.

Q: Zameen bechne par TDS kab katta hai?

▼

A: Sale value ₹50 lakh ya usse zyada hone par buyer ko Section 194-IA ke tahat 1% TDS katna hota hai (Form 16B). Yeh aapki final tax liability mein adjust hota hai.

Q: Agar reinvest karne ka time nahi mila to kya karein?

▼

A: ITR due date (aam taur par 31 July) tak Capital Gains Account Scheme (CGAS) account mein amount jama kar dein aur exemption claim karein. Baad mein 2-3 saal ke andar use karna hoga.
