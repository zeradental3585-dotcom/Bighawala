📖 Waris Praman Patra Kya Hai aur Kab Zaroori Hota Hai?

Waris Praman Patra (Legal Heir Certificate) ek administrative dastavez hai jo circle/anchal office dwara jaari kiya jaata hai. Yeh pramanit karta hai ki mrityu ke baad us vyakti ke seedhe kanooni waris (jaise patni/pati, bete-betiyan, maata-pita) kaun hain.

Kab Zaroorat Padti Hai:

  • Zameen Mutation (Dakhil Kharij): Agar zameen ke maalik ki mrityu ho chuki hai, toh unke naam se hataakar sabhi warison ke naam Jamabandi mein darj karne ke liye.
  • Sarkari Naukri Benefits: Marne wale sarkari karmchari ke pension, gratuity ya provident fund (PF) claim karne ke liye.
  • Bank Accounts: Mrit vyakti ke bank account se paisa nikalne ya transfer karne ke liye (kai baar Succession Certificate bhi maanga jaata hai).
  • Sarkari Yojana Labh: Kisan Samman Nidhi, Kisan Credit Card jaisi yojanaon mein warison ke naam update karne ke liye.

⚠️ Zaroori Baat: Online Apply Nahi Hota

RTPS Bihar portal par kai anya praman patra (jaise Jaati, Aay, Nivas) online milte hain — lekin Waris Praman Patra ke liye abhi tak koi poori tarah online application ka vikalp nahi hai. Aapko apne Anchal (Block) ke RTPS counter par jaakar offline application jama karni padti hai.

⚖️ Waris Praman Patra vs Vanshavali vs Succession Certificate

Bahut log in teenon dastavezon ko ek hi samajh lete hain — jo galat hai. Fark samjhein:

Dastavez Kahan se Milta Hai Mukhya Upyog
Waris Praman Patra Anchal / RTPS Counter (Circle Office) Pension, PF, gratuity, bank claims
Vanshavali Gram Panchayat (Secretary + Sarpanch verified) Zameen Dakhil Kharij, purani zameen ka batwara
Succession Certificate Civil Court (District Judge) Vivadit maamle, movable assets (shares, deposits)

💡 Zameen mutation ke liye zyaadatar Anchal office Vanshavali maangte hain, na ki Waris Praman Patra. Dono ek saath rakhna sabse surakshit hai.

📋 Zaroori Documents

✅ Mrityu Praman Patra (Death Certificate)
✅ Sabhi warison ke Aadhar Card
✅ Notary-verified Affidavit
✅ Gram Panchayat se verified Vanshavali
✅ Address Proof (Ration card/Voter ID)
✅ Applicant ki Passport size photo

🛠️ Apply Kaise Karein — Step by Step

  1. 1

    Vanshavali Taiyaar Karayein

    Village/Panchayat Secretary aur Sarpanch se parivar ki Vanshavali verify karayein. Vanshavali Generator Tool yahan use karein →

  2. 2

    Notary Affidavit Banwayein

    Warison ki list aur rishta batate hue ek affidavit notary se attest karayein.

  3. 3

    Anchal / RTPS Counter Jayein

    Sabhi documents ke saath apne block/anchal office ke RTPS counter par application form jama karein.

  4. 4

    Verification aur Enquiry

    Anchal karmchari documents aur zaroorat padne par field verification karte hain.

  5. 5

    Certificate Prapt Karein

    2-4 hafton mein saamanya taur par Waris Praman Patra jaari ho jaata hai.
