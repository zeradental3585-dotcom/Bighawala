💻 Status Check Karne Ka Tareeka

  1. emutation.bihar.gov.in open karein
  2. Homepage par "LPC Application Status" ya "Mutation Status" option select karein (jo aapko chahiye)
  3. Apna District aur Anchal select karein
  4. Application Number ya Case Number enter karein (agar pata ho), ya Jamabandi/Registration number se search karein
  5. "Search" par click karke current status dekhein

⚠️ Common Problems aur Solutions

Solution: Apply karte waqt aaye SMS ya email mein application number check karein. Agar nahi mila, registered mobile number se portal login karke "My Applications" section dekhein, ya Anchal office se sampark karein.
Solution: Dakhil Kharij ka sarkari cycle 45 din ka hota hai. Agar deadline paar ho gayi ho, Bihar Bhumi Jan Shikayat portal (biharbhumiplus.bihar.gov.in/pg/) par shikayat darj karein.
Solution: Rejection reason portal par ya Anchal office se pata karein — aksar documents incomplete hone ya record mismatch ki wajah se hota hai. Sahi documents ke saath dobara apply karein.
