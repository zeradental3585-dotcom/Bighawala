Khatiyan Bihar 2026 | खतियान क्या होता है और Jamabandi से क्या फर्क है?

💡 Summary Guide 2026

Khatiyan Bihar ke land record system ka sabse purana document hai:

✅ Khatiyan = Survey ke waqt (CS/RS) ka one-time base record

✅ Jamabandi = Usi Khatiyan se bana current, updated ownership register

Current Jamabandi/RoR Bihar Bhumi official portal par FREE mein online dekh sakte hain: land.bihar.gov.in/Ror2025/RoR.aspx

📖 Khatiyan kya hota hai?

📜 Purani Janamptri (Birth Certificate) Analogy:
Khatiyan zameen ki janamptri jaisa hai — jaise aapka birth certificate ek baar banta hai aur usi se aage aadhar card, passport jaise updated documents banate hain, waise hi Khatiyan ek baar survey ke waqt banta hai aur usi se aage Jamabandi (Register-II) taiyaar hoti hai.

Khatiyan ke Mukhya Points:

  • One-Time Document: Khatiyan land settlement/survey (jaise CS, RS ya Special Survey) ke waqt ek baar banta hai.
  • No Regular Update: Khatiyan mein baad mein hone wale ownership changes update nahi hote — sirf Jamabandi update hoti hai.
  • Base Document: Jamabandi/Register-II is Khatiyan record se hi taiyaar ki jaati hai.
  • Legal Proof: Purani chain of ownership prove karne ke liye Khatiyan sabse authentic historical document mana jaata hai.
  • Survey-wise Naam: Alag-alag survey period ke Khatiyan ko CS Khatiyan, RS Khatiyan ya SS Khatiyan kaha jaata hai.

🗂️ CS Khatiyan aur RS Khatiyan mein kya fark hai?

🕰️ Purane Survey Periods:
Bihar mein zameen ki survey alag-alag dashkon mein hui hai, aur har survey ka apna Khatiyan record hota hai. Isliye ek hi zameen ke kayi "generations" ke Khatiyan mil sakte hain.

Survey Types ki Chain:

  • CS Khatiyan (Cadastral Survey): Sabse purana aur mool survey record, jo British-era mein shuru hua tha.
  • RS Khatiyan (Revisional Survey): CS Khatiyan ke baad hui revised/updated survey ka record.
  • SS Khatiyan (Special Survey): Kai zilon mein hal ke saalon mein hui Special Survey ka naya record.
  • Sequence: Aam taur par CS → RS → SS is order mein survey hui hai, aur har naya survey purane ko update/replace karta hai.

⚖️ Khatiyan aur Jamabandi mein kya fark hai?

Dono ke antar ko is simple comparison table se samjhein — bahut log inhe ek hi samajh lete hain, jo galat hai:

Feature / Viseshta Khatiyan (खतियान) Jamabandi (जमाबंदी)
Kya Hai? Survey ke waqt ka base historical record Current, updated ownership register (Register-II)
Update hota hai? Nahi — ek baar bana, permanent rehta hai Haan — Dakhil Kharij ke baad regularly update hoti hai
Kab Banta Hai? Land Survey / Settlement ke waqt (CS, RS, SS) Khatiyan se, aur har mutation ke baad
Online Availability Kuch zilon mein digitized copy, sabhi jagah nahi Bihar Bhumi portal par sabhi jagah available
Main Use Purani ownership chain aur court evidence Current malkiyat, loan, registry ke liye
Online Portal Link Anchal / DLRS record room land.bihar.gov.in

  2. Apna District, Anchal, Halka aur Mauja select karein
  3. Khata Number, Khesra Number ya Malik ke Naam se search karein
  4. Current digitized Jamabandi (jo Khatiyan se derive hui hai) screen par dikhegi

PORTAL 2 — Purana Khatiyan

Anchal / Circle Office Record Room

  1. Apne local Anchal (Circle) Office ke record room jayein

  3. District, Mauja aur Khata/Khesra number batayein

  5. Kuch zilon mein online digitized Khatiyan bhi available hai — apne DLRS office se pata karein

⚡ Khatiyan kab kaam aata hai?

Bihar mein purani zameen se jude in kaamon mein Khatiyan record ki zaroorat padti hai:

⚖️

Court Case & Vivad

Zameen vivad ya adalat mein purani malkiyat prove karne ke liye original Khatiyan sabse strong evidence hota hai.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Purkhon ki Zameen (Batwara)

Purane parivarik zameen ka batwara karte waqt Khatiyan se chain of ownership samjhi jaati hai.

📋

Dakhil Kharij Verification

Mutation application mein purani chain of title verify karne ke liye CO/RO Khatiyan reference maang sakte hain.

🏦

Bank Loan (Old Property)

Purani property par loan lete waqt bank chain of title mein Khatiyan tak record maang sakta hai.

💰

Zameen Registry

Purani zameen bechte waqt registry office Khatiyan se lekar current Jamabandi tak title chain check karta hai.

📑

Record Correction

Jamabandi mein galti sudharne ke liye kabhi-kabhi original Khatiyan se cross-verify karna padta hai.
