वारिस स्व-घोषणा शपथ पत्र

Legal Heir Self-Declaration Affidavit

Jab zameen malik ki mrityu ho jaaye aur unke naam ki zameen ko warison ke naam par Dakhil Kharij (mutation) karwana ho, ya RTPS se Waris Praman Patra banwaana ho — tab yeh affidavit chahiye hota hai. Ismein sabhi jeevit warison ke naam, mritak se sambandh aur pata ek table mein darj karna hota hai.

📋 Kahan istemal hota hai: Dakhil Kharij (inheritance), RTPS Waris Praman Patra
📝 Ismein kya hai: Warison ki table, no-will declaration, verification clause

Yeh wahi affidavit hai jo "bina bataye zameen bech di" jaisi situation ko future mein hone se rok sakta hai.

📋 Kahan istemal hota hai: Zameen bikri se pehle, partition, registry

2️⃣ Stamp Paper Par Print Karayein

Sahi jaankari bharne ke baad, ise apne local stamp vendor se le kar sahi value ke non-judicial stamp paper par print/likhwayein.

3️⃣ Notary Se Attest Karayein

Apne aur (agar zaroori ho to) sabhi sah-hissedaron ke saamne local Notary Public ke paas jaakar hastakshar/attest karwayein.

4️⃣ Application Ke Saath Jama Karein

Attested affidavit ko apne Dakhil Kharij/RTPS/Parimarjan application ke saath online upload ya Anchal office mein jama karein.

❓ FAQ — Zameen Shapath Patra Format

Q: Kya yeh shapath patra format legally valid hain?

▼

A: Yeh standard affidavit structure par based starting draft hain. Sahi stamp paper par print karke, blanks bharkar Notary se attest karwana zaroori hai. Mahatvapurna transaction se pehle ek advocate se salaah lein.

Q: Shapath patra ke liye kitne rupaye ke stamp paper ki zaroorat hoti hai?

▼

A: Aam taur par ₹10 se ₹100 ke beech, lekin application aur zile ke hisaab se alag ho sakti hai — apne local stamp vendor se confirm karein.

Q: Waris Declaration Affidavit kab zaroorat padti hai?

▼

A: Zameen malik ki mrityu ke baad Dakhil Kharij ya RTPS Waris Praman Patra ke liye — sabhi jeevit warison ko sabit karne ke liye.

Q: Co-Sharer Sahmati Shapath Patra ka istemal kab karein?

▼

A: Jab sanyukt/ancestral zameen mein se koi hissa bikna ho, tab sabhi sah-hissedaron se likhit sahmati lene ke liye — future vivad se bachne ke liye.

Q: Naam Sudhar Shapath Patra kis kaam aata hai?

▼

A: Jamabandi/Khatiyan mein naam ki spelling mismatch sudharne ke liye — Parimarjan Plus, LPC ya Registry application ke saath.

Q: Kya yeh templates download karne ke liye paise dene padte hain?

▼

A: Nahi, teeno format bilkul free hain — koi login ya payment nahi.
