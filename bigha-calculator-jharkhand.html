📍 Zaroori Baat — Jharkhand Ka Bigha Bihar Se Alag Hai

Jharkhand pehle Bihar ka hi hissa tha (2000 tak), lekin iska matlab yeh nahi ki dono ka bigha same size ka hai. Katha bhi ek local unit hai jiska size Jharkhand ke andar hi district se district badalta hai:

✅ Standard Katha (Ranchi/Hazaribagh aur zyada tar Jharkhand)

Sabse zyada quote kiya jaane wala figure: 1 Katha = 4 Decimal = 1,742.4 sq ft. Yeh Bihar ke Katha (1,361.25 sq ft) se bada hai.

⚠️ Kuch Districts Mein Alag Size

Palamu, Deoghar aur Santhal Pargana belt jaise kuch districts mein Katha ka size ismein diye gaye standard se kaafi alag ho sakta hai — sources 720 sqft se lekar 2,614 sqft tak ka range batate hain.

🎯 Sabse bharosemand tareeka: Apni zameen ka exact area jharbhoomi.nic.in par Khatiyan ya Register-II se check karein — waha area official taur par Decimal/Acre/Hectare mein darj hota hai, Bigha/Katha mein nahi.

⚖️ Jharkhand Mein Zameen Kharidne Se Pehle — CNT/SPT Act Zaroor Jaanein

Yeh Jharkhand ki sabse zaroori aur alag baat hai jo Bihar mein apply nahi hoti. Chotanagpur Tenancy Act (CNT) 1908 aur Santhal Parganas Tenancy Act (SPT) 1949 ke tahat, Scheduled Tribe (ST) raiyat ki zameen kisi bhi non-tribal ko bina Deputy Commissioner (DC) ki poorv likhit anumati ke transfer nahi ki ja sakti — aisa koi bhi transfer (bikri, gift, etc.) legally void mana jata hai.

CNT Act Kahan Lagu Hai

North Chotanagpur, South Chotanagpur aur Palamu division — jaise Ranchi, Khunti, Gumla, Hazaribagh, Dhanbad, Bokaro, Palamu, Latehar.

SPT Act Kahan Lagu Hai

Santhal Pargana belt — Dumka, Jamtara, Sahibganj, Godda, Deoghar, Pakur districts.

📌 Zameen kharidne ya inherit karne se pehle Khatiyan mein zaroor check karein ki woh "Scheduled Tribe" raiyat ki zameen to nahi hai. Agar hai, aur aap non-tribal hain, to DC office se poorv anumati ke bina koi bhi transaction legally invalid ho sakta hai.

❓ FAQ — Jharkhand Bigha Calculator

Q: Jharkhand mein 1 Bigha kitna Katha hota hai?

▼

A: Zyada tar hisso mein 1 Bigha = 20 Katha hota hai, lekin Katha ka exact size district se district badal sakta hai — apne district mein confirm karein.

Q: Jharkhand mein 1 Katha kitne square feet ka hota hai?

▼

A: Ranchi aur zyada tar Jharkhand mein sabse zyada quote kiya jaane wala figure 1 Katha = 4 Decimal = 1,742.4 sqft hai — lekin location ke hisab se 720 se 2,614 sqft tak bhi ho sakta hai.

Q: Kya Jharkhand ka Bigha aur Bihar ka Bigha same hai?

▼

A: Nahi. Jharkhand ka standard bigha (34,848 sqft) Bihar ke standard bigha (27,225 sqft) se bada hai, chahe Jharkhand pehle Bihar ka hi hissa tha.

Q: Jharkhand ke official land record mein zameen kis unit mein likhi hoti hai?

▼

A: Khatiyan/registry records mein area officially Decimal, Acre ya Hectare mein darj hota hai. Apni exact area jharbhoomi.nic.in par check karein.

Q: Kya koi bhi Jharkhand mein tribal zameen khareed sakta hai?

▼

A: Nahi. CNT/SPT Act ke tahat ST raiyat ki zameen non-tribal ko bina DC ki poorv anumati ke transfer nahi ki ja sakti — aisa transfer void mana jata hai.

Q: CNT Act aur SPT Act kaha-kaha lagu hote hain?

▼

A: CNT Act North/South Chotanagpur aur Palamu division mein, SPT Act Dumka, Jamtara, Sahibganj, Godda, Deoghar, Pakur (Santhal Pargana) mein lagu hota hai.
