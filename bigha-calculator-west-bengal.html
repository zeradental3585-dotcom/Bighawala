❓ FAQ — West Bengal Bigha Calculator

Q: West Bengal mein 1 Bigha kitna Katha hota hai?

▼

A: West Bengal ke standard unit system mein 1 Bigha = 20 Katha hota hai — yeh figure Kolkata samet zyada tar West Bengal mein consistently use hota hai.

Q: West Bengal mein 1 Katha kitne square feet ka hota hai?

▼

A: 1 Katha = 16 Chatak = 720 sqft hota hai — Bihar ke Katha (1,361.25 sqft) se kaafi chhota.

Q: 1 Chatak kitne square feet ka hota hai?

▼

A: 1 Chatak = 45 sqft hota hai — chhote plot ya flat ka area batane ke liye aksar Chatak use hota hai.

Q: Kya West Bengal ka Bigha aur Bihar ka Bigha same size ka hota hai?

▼

A: Nahi. West Bengal ka standard Bigha (14,400 sqft) Bihar ke standard Bigha (27,225 sqft) se lagbhag aadha hai. Kabhi bhi ek state ke figure ko doosre state mein waise hi maan kar transaction na karein.

Q: West Bengal ke official land record mein zameen kis unit mein likhi hoti hai?

▼

A: Khatian (RS-LR record) mein area officially Acre, Decimal aur Sq Ft mein darj hota hai. Apni exact area banglarbhumi.gov.in par check karein.

Q: West Bengal mein zameen ka record online kaise check karein?

▼

A: banglarbhumi.gov.in par District, Block aur Mouza chunkar Khatian ya Plot (Dag) number se record check karein. Mutation ko Bangla mein "Namjari" ya "Dakhil-Kharij" kaha jata hai.
