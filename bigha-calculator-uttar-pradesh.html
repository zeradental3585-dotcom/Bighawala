📍 Pehle Yeh Samjhein — UP Mein Bigha Ek Jaisa Nahi Hai

Bihar ke bigha ke ulat, Uttar Pradesh mein bigha ka size poore state mein ek jaisa nahi hai — yeh district se district badalta hai:

✅ Standard/Pucca Bigha (Zyada Tar UP)

Purvanchal (Gorakhpur, Varanasi, Azamgarh), Awadh (Lucknow) aur UP ke zyada tar revenue records mein 27,225 sq ft (20 Biswa) wala bigha use hota hai — bilkul Bihar ke bigha jaisa.

⚠️ Kuch Western UP Districts Mein Chhota Bigha

Meerut, Muzaffarnagar, Bulandshahr jaise kuch districts mein local taur par bahut chhota bigha (jaise 5-Biswa wala) bhi prachalit hai — alag-alag sources iska size alag-alag batate hain, isliye hum ek fix number nahi de sakte.

🎯 Sabse bharosemand tareeka: Apni zameen ka exact area upbhulekh.gov.in par Khasra/Khatauni number se check karein — waha area Hectare mein official taur par darj hota hai (1 Hectare = lagbhag 1,07,639 sq ft). Agar aap Western UP mein hain aur local bigha ka size confirm karna hai, to apne Tehsil ke Lekhpal se poochhein.

🤔 UP Mein Bigha Har Jagah Alag Kyun Hai?

Bigha ek colonial-era, sthaniya (local) mapan unit hai — koi ek central authority ne isse kabhi poore Uttar Pradesh ke liye standardize nahi kiya. Har ilaake ne apne historical revenue-survey tareeke ke hisab se apna bigha define kiya, isliye aaj bhi Purvanchal, Awadh aur Western UP mein alag-alag size prachalit hain. Isi wajah se UP sarkar ke official Khatauni records legally Hectare/Sq Metre mein hi rakhe jate hain — bigha sirf logon ki roz-marra ki baat-cheet ke liye reh gaya hai.

❓ FAQ — UP Bigha Calculator

Q: Uttar Pradesh mein 1 Bigha kitna Biswa hota hai?

▼

A: Standard/pucca bigha mein 1 Bigha = 20 Biswa hota hai (Purvanchal, Awadh, zyada tar UP). Kuch Western UP districts mein alag, chhota bigha bhi prachalit hai — apne district mein confirm karein.

Q: 1 Pucca Bigha kitne square feet ka hota hai?

▼

A: 27,225 sq ft = 3,025 sq yard (gaj) = 0.625 Acre = lagbhag 0.253 Hectare — bilkul Bihar ke standard bigha jaisa.

Q: Kya UP ke har jile mein Bigha ka size same hota hai?

▼

A: Nahi. Purvanchal/Awadh mein bada bigha (27,225 sqft) chalta hai, jabki kuch Western UP districts mein chhota bigha prachalit hai jiska exact size sources mein alag-alag bataya jata hai — local confirm karna zaroori hai.

Q: UP ke official land record (Khatauni) mein zameen kis unit mein likhi hoti hai?

▼

A: Khatauni records mein area legally Hectare/Sq Metre mein darj hota hai — Bigha sirf colloquial istemaal ke liye hai. Apni exact area upbhulekh.gov.in par check karein.

Q: Kya UP ka Purvanchal Bigha aur Bihar ka Bigha same hai?

▼

A: Haan, dono 27,225 sqft ke barabar hain — bas sub-unit ka naam alag hai (UP: Biswa/Biswansi, Bihar: Katha/Dhur/Dhurki).

Q: 1 Biswansi kitne square feet ka hota hai?

▼

A: Standard measurement mein 1 Biswansi lagbhag 68.06 sq ft ka hota hai (1 Biswa ka 1/20 hissa).
