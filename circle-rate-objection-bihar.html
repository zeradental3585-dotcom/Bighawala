Circle Rate (MVR) Galat Lage Toh Kya Karein? Bihar 2026

💡 Summary Guide 2026

Agar aapki zameen ka MVR/Circle Rate galat ya bahut purana lagta hai, toh ye steps follow karein:

1️⃣ Pehle official portal par current rate double-check karein

2️⃣ Fir District Registration Office mein likhit representation dein

📖 MVR/Circle Rate Kya Hai?

Bihar mein MVR (Minimum Value Register) hi aam bhasha mein "Circle Rate" kehlaata hai. Yeh sarkar dwara nirdharit ek minimum floor price hai — iske neeche kisi bhi zameen ki registry legally nahi ho sakti, chahe kharidar-vikreta aapas mein kam paison mein sahmat kyun na hon.

🛠️ Rate Galat Lage Toh Kya Karein?

⚠️ Honest Note: Bihar mein MVR sudhaar ke liye ek single, samarpit online "objection form" filhaal spasht roop se available nahi hai. Neeche diye gaye steps hi practically sabse behtar rasta hain — apne local Sub-Registrar office se current procedure zaroor confirm karein.
  1. 1

    Rate Double-Check Karein

    Bhumi Jankari portal par registration office, circle naam, thana code aur land type sahi se daalkar exact current MVR verify karein — kai baar galat entry ki wajah se hi rate galat lagta hai.

  2. 2

    Likhit Representation Taiyaar Karein

    Apni zameen ki details (khata/khesra, mauja, thana) aur aapke concern (jaise rate bahut purana ya galat hai) likhkar ek application banayein.

  3. 3

    District Registration Office Mein Jama Karein

    Apne District Sub-Registrar Office mein application submit karein, ya zaroorat padne par state-level Inspector General of Registration office ko likhein.

  4. 4

    Registry Ke Waqt Valuation Dispute

    Agar registry karte waqt hi valuation ko lekar sawaal uthta hai, toh Sub-Registrar Registration Act ke pravadhano ke antargat spot verification ki prakriya apna sakte hain — jismein aapko apna paksh rakhne ka mauka milega.
