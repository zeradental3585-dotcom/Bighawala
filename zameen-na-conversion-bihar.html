Zameen NA Conversion Bihar 2026 | कृषि से गैर-कृषि जमीन कैसे बदलें?

💡 Summary Guide 2026

Krishi zameen par ghar/dukaan banane se pehle NA Conversion legally zaroori hai:

🏛️ Approval Authority: Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO)

💰 Fees: ~10% market value (notified areas mein)

Legal aadhaar: Bihar Agriculture Land (Conversion for Non-Agriculture Purposes) Act, 2010

📖 NA Conversion Kya Hai aur Kyun Zaroori Hai?

Bihar mein zameen do prakar ki hoti hai — Agricultural (Krishi) aur Non-Agricultural (Gair-Krishi/NA). Agar aapki zameen abhi Jamabandi mein "Agricultural" darj hai aur aap usmein ghar, dukaan, factory ya kisi bhi commercial/residential nirman ki yojana bana rahe hain, toh pehle usko kanooni roop se NA (Non-Agricultural) mein convert karana zaroori hai.

⚠️ Bina NA conversion ke banaya gaya construction technically unauthorized maana ja sakta hai, aur building plan/naksha paas karana mushkil ho jaata hai.

🛠️ Conversion Process — Step by Step

  1. 1

    Documents Taiyaar Karein

    Current Jamabandi, Khatiyan, LPC, naksha (site plan) aur tax rasid ikattha karein.

  2. 2

    SDO Office Mein Application Dein

    Bihar Agriculture Land (Conversion) Act, 2010 ki Section 3(2) ke antargat apne Sub-Division ke SDO office mein application submit karein.

  3. 3

    Site Inspection & Verification

    Revenue karmchari zameen ka field verification karte hain aur revenue dues check karte hain.

  4. 4

    Conversion Fee Jama Karein

    Market value ka lagbhag 10% conversion fee bharni hoti hai (notified areas mein).

  5. 5

    NA Permission Letter

    Niyamानुसार 90 dinon mein SDO manzoori ya rejection deta hai. Manzoori ke baad Jamabandi mein "NA" darj kiya jaata hai.

⏱️ Reality Check: Kanooni timeline 90 din hai, lekin adhoore documents, purane revenue dues ya bureaucratic process ki wajah se practically 6-12 mahine ya usse zyada lag sakta hai. Apne SDO/Anchal office se current status regularly follow up karte rahein.

📋 Zaroori Documents

✅ Current Jamabandi/RoR copy
✅ Khatiyan copy
✅ LPC (Land Possession Certificate)
✅ Zameen ka naksha (Site Plan)
✅ Rajasva rasid (Tax Receipt)
✅ Ownership proof (Registry/Dakhil Kharij)
