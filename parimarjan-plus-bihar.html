📖 Parimarjan Plus kya hai?

📝 Simple Definition:
Parimarjan Plus, Bihar sarkar ke Rajasva evam Bhumi Sudhar Vibhag (Department of Revenue and Land Reforms) ka official portal hai jiske through aap apni digitized Jamabandi mein reh gayi galtiyan khud online sudhar sakte hain — bina Anchal office ke chakkar lagaye.

Parimarjan Plus ke Mukhya Points:

  • Sirf Digitized Jamabandi ke liye: Yeh portal sirf computerized/digitized Jamabandi record mein hui clerical galtiyon ke liye hai.
  • Online Self-Service: Registered mobile number se login karke khud apna correction application file kar sakte hain.
  • Anchal Adhikari (CO) Approval Zaroori: Application submit karne ke baad CO isse verify karke approve ya comment ke saath wapas karte hain.
  • Shapath Patra Anivarya: Har application ke saath ek self-declaration affidavit dena zaroori hai.

🛠️ Parimarjan Plus mein kya-kya sudhar ho sakta hai?

Official portal ke anusaar, digitized Jamabandi mein in categories ki galtiyan sudhari ja sakti hain:

🧑

Raiyat Vivaran (Name/Address)

Raiyat ka naam, pita ka naam, ya pata (address) mein likhawat ki galti sudhrein.

🔢

Khata, Khesra aur Rakba

Khata number, khesra number, rakba (area) ya chauhaddi mein hui typing/digitization galti thik karein.

💰

Lagan (Land Rent)

Bhu-lagan (annual land rent) ki raashi ya category se judi galti sudhaari ja sakti hai.

📜

Jaati Praman Patra

Record mein darj jaati (caste) vivaran ki galti caste certificate ke aadhar par sudhrein.

📐

Kul Kshetrafal

Zameen ke total area (kul kshetrafal) mein reh gayi ganana ki galti sudhaari ja sakti hai.

🗂️

Chhooti Hui Jamabandi

Computerization ke waqt digitize hone se reh gayi (missed) purani Jamabandi ko bhi is portal se add karwaya ja sakta hai.

📋 Zaroori Documents (Correction ke Prakar ke Anusaar)

Official User Manual ke anusaar, application file karne se pehle Shapath Patra (mandatory) ke saath, sudhar ke prakar ke hisaab se yeh saakshya (evidence) taiyaar rakhein:

Sudhar ka Prakar Zaroori Document (Yadi Aavashyak Ho)
Shapath Patra (Sabhi Applications) Website par uplabdh Shapath Patra bharkar — anivarya (mandatory)
Raiyat Vivaran mein Sudhar Lagan raseed, Dakhil-Kharij shuddhi patra, Jamabandi panji copy, Dakhil-Kharij aadesh
Jaati Praman Patra mein Sudhar Jaati praman patra
Pata (Address) mein Sudhar Aadhaar Card, Bijli Bill, Ration Card, Driving License, Passport ya Voter ID (koi ek)
Lagan mein Sudhar Lagan raseed
Bhoomi Vivaran mein Sudhar (Khata/Khesra/Rakba) CS/RS/Chakbandi Khatiyan, Dakhil-Kharij shuddhi patra ya aadesh, Bhoomi dakhil-kabja praman patra, Sarkari bhoomi parcha abhilekh (agar laagu ho)

Note: Har document tabhi maangi jaati hai jab wo aapke specific sudhar ke case mein applicable ho ("यदि आवश्यक हो").

📝 Naya Application Kaise File Karein? (Official Steps)

1

biharbhumi.bihar.gov.in official portal kholein.

2

"परिमार्जन" par click karein. Agar aap pehle se registered hain toh apne mobile number se login karein (naye user pehle registration karein), phir "Parimarjan Plus" chunein.

3

Application ke prakar mein "डिजिटल जमाबंदी में सुधार करें" ke antargat "Rectification in Old Jamabandi" chunein.

4

Jis Jamabandi mein sudhar chahiye, use search karein.

5

Search ki gayi Jamabandi ke aage diye gaye (→) button par click karein — poora vivaran khulega. Naam, pita ka naam, pata, khata, khesra, rakba, chauhaddi ya lagan — jis section mein sudhar chahiye use "YES" karke us vivaran ko update karein.

6

Sabhi badlaav darj karne ke baad sambandhit saakshya (documents) upload karein aur "Preview" button par click karke apna bhara hua vivaran dobara check karein.

7

Sab sahi lage toh Shapath Patra upload karke application ko finalize karein (galti ho toh Edit button se pehle theek karein), aur Final Submit karein.

8

Final Submit ke baad ek Paavti Sankhya (Acknowledgement Number) milegi — isi se aap apne application ki status track kar sakte hain. Isse sambhal kar rakhein.

⚠️ Agar Anchal Adhikari (CO) Aapatti Kare Toh?

Application submit karne ke baad Anchal Adhikari (CO) usko verify karte hain. Agar unhe application mein koi aapatti (objection) hoti hai, toh woh apne comment ke saath application aapko wapas kar sakte hain. Aisa returned application aapko apne Parimarjan Plus home screen par dikhega — jahan CO ke comment ke anusaar zaroori sudhar karke usi upar diye gaye process se dobara submit kiya ja sakta hai.
