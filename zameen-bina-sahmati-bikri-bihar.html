🔍 Pehle Confirm Karein — Kya Yeh Sach Mein "Ancestral" Property Hai?

Neeche diya gaya poora guide ancestral (undivided coparcenary) property ke liye hai — yani zameen jo kai peedhiyon se family mein chali aa rahi hai aur kabhi formally baati (partition) nahi gayi. Agar zameen aapke pita ne khud kamayi hui / khareedi hui hai (self-acquired), to woh apni marzi se kisi ko bhi de/bech sakte hain — us case mein coparcenary rules apply nahi hote (sirf virasat/will ke niyam apply honge).

✅ Yeh Guide Aapke Liye Hai Agar:

Zameen dada/pardada ke zamaane se chali aa rahi hai, kabhi formal baatwara nahi hua, aur aap (ya aapke parents) us mein legal heir/coparcener hain.

ℹ️ Agar Zameen Self-Acquired Hai:

Owner (jaise aapke pita) apni marzi se bech/gift kar sakte hain — sirf will/wasiyat ya virasat ke rules apply honge, coparcenary rights nahi.

⚖️ Kanoon Kya Kehta Hai — Kya Yeh Sale Valid Hai?

Ancestral (undivided) property mein har coparcener (bhai, behen, chacha, etc.) ka apna ek undivided share hota hai — lekin uski koi fix, alag-se-nishaan-lagi hui jagah nahi hoti jab tak formal baatwara na ho. Iska matlab:

  • → Koi bhi coparcener sirf apna khud ka share bech sakta hai, poori zameen ya kisi aur ka hissa nahi.
  • → Agar woh poori zameen ya ek specific portion "meri hai" bolkar bech deta hai, to woh sale sirf uske apne share tak valid hai — baaki coparcener ke against woh sale invalid/inoperative maani jaati hai.
  • → Buyer ko sirf utna hi mila jitna seller ke paas legally tha — baaki share ka haq aapka hi rehta hai.
📌 Ek Exception: Agar seller family ka "Karta" hai (sabse senior/managing member) aur usne "legal necessity" (jaise family ka karza chukana, medical emergency) ke liye becha hai, to Supreme Court (Sushil Kumar vs Ram Prakash, 1988) ke anusar woh baaki coparcener ki sahmati ke bina bhi bech sakta hai — lekin buyer ko yeh saabit karna hoga ki usne genuinely "due diligence" karke legal necessity verify ki thi.
👩 Behnon Ke Liye Zaroori Baat: Hindu Succession (Amendment) Act 2005 ke baad, betiyon ko bhi beton jaisa hi poora coparcenary right milta hai — sirf partition maangne ka claim nahi. Yeh right tab bhi lagu hota hai jab father 2005 se pehle gujar chuke hon, jab tak beti 9 September 2005 ko zinda thi (Vineeta Sharma vs Rakesh Sharma, Supreme Court, 2020).

⏳ Kitna Time Hai — Kya Ab Bahut Der Ho Gayi?

Yeh sawaal sabse zyada pucha jata hai — khaas taur par jab kisi ko saal ya das saal baad pata chalta hai ki unke gaon ki zameen bik chuki hai. Do situations hain:

Agar Aapne Khud Sign Kiya Tha (fraud/dabaav mein)

Aapko sale deed "cancel" karwani hogi — Limitation Act ke Article 59 ke tahat, fraud pata chalne ki tarikh se 3 saal ke andar suit file karna hoga.

Agar Aapke Signature Liye Hi Nahi Gaye

Woh deed aapke share ke against shuru se hi void hai — koi "cancellation" ki zaroorat nahi. 2025 ke Supreme Court faisle (Shanti Devi vs Jagan Devi) ke anusar aapke paas possession/title claim karne ke liye Article 65 ki 12 saal wali limitation lagu hoti hai.

Phir bhi: jitni jaldi action lein utna behtar — gawah, documents aur record mazboot rehta hai, aur buyer ke aage transfer karne se pehle aap rok sakte hain.

✈️ Agar Aap Bihar Nahi Aa Sakte

Aapko baar-baar Bihar aane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Ek local, bharosemand advocate ko Special Power of Attorney (SPA) dekar unhe apni taraf se objection file karne, mutation mein hastakshep karne, aur court case chalane ka poora adhikar de sakte hain. SPA ko notarize ya registered karwa kar advocate ko bhej dein.

❓ FAQ — Bina Sahmati Zameen Bikri

Q: Kya bhai/rishtedar meri sahmati ke bina ancestral zameen bech sakte hain?

▼

A: Nahi. Har coparcener sirf apna khud ka undivided share hi bech sakta hai — poori zameen ya kisi aur ka hissa nahi, jab tak formal partition na ho chuka ho. Agar poori zameen bech di gayi hai, to sale sirf seller ke apne share tak valid hai.

Q: Kya beti/behen ko bhai jitna hi ancestral property mein haq hai?

▼

A: Haan, 2005 ke amendment ke baad betiyon ko bhi poora coparcenary right milta hai, chahe father 2005 se pehle gujar gaye hon (bashart beti 9 Sept 2005 ko zinda thi).

Q: Agar zameen registry ho chuki hai to kya ab bahut der ho gayi hai?

▼

A: Zaroori nahi. Agar aap deed ke party nahi the, deed aapke share ke against shuru se void hai, aur 2025 ke SC faisle ke anusar 12 saal (Article 65) tak time hai — 3 saal wali cancellation limitation (Article 59) lagu nahi hoti.

Q: Main Bihar se bahar rehta hoon — kya main office jaaye bina case file karwa sakta hoon?

▼

A: Haan, ek local advocate ko Special Power of Attorney (SPA) dekar unhein objection file karne aur case chalane ka adhikar de sakte hain.

Q: Dakhil kharij (mutation) mein online objection kaise dalein?

▼

A: parimarjan.bihar.gov.in ya emutation.bihar.gov.in par status check karein aur "pending"/"notice issued" dikhne par apna proof lagakar likhit objection submit karein.

Q: Agar rishtedar ne fake signature ya fake documents use kiye hain to kya karein?

▼

A: Turant FIR darj karayein — BNS Section 336 (forgery), 340 (forged document use) aur 318 (cheating) ke tahat.

Q: Kya sirf legal notice bhejne se sale ruk jayegi?

▼

A: Notice khud automatically nahi rokti, lekin buyer ko risk se aagah karti hai. Poori tarah rokne ke liye court se temporary injunction lena sabse mazboot tareeka hai.
