Halka aur Mauja Kya Hota Hai? Bihar Revenue System 2026

💡 Summary Guide 2026

Bihar mein zameen dhoondhne ke liye do zaroori revenue units samajhna padta hai:

✅ Halka = Halka Karamchari ke charge wali smallest revenue unit (kai gaon ka samuh)

✅ Mauja = Revenue village — Jamabandi/Khatiyan/naksha ki basic unit (aapka gaon)

RoR search karte waqt Bihar Bhumi portal par yeh dono select karne padte hain: land.bihar.gov.in/Ror2025/RoR.aspx

🏘️ Halka kya hota hai?

🏢 Beat/Zone Analogy:
Halka ko aap ek local beat ya zone ki tarah samajh sakte hain jismein ek revenue officer — Halka Karamchari — kai gaanvon ke land records dekhta hai. Yeh Anchal (Circle) ke andar sabse chhoti revenue administrative unit hai.

Halka ke Mukhya Points:

  • Smallest Unit: Anchal ke neeche Bihar revenue administration ki sabse chhoti unit Halka hoti hai.
  • Halka Karamchari: Har Halka ek Revenue Karamchari (Halka Karamchari) ke charge mein hota hai.
  • Kai Mauja: Ek Halka mein aam taur par kai Mauja (revenue villages) shamil hote hain.
  • Ground-Level Verification: Dakhil Kharij, Jamabandi verification aur field-level record checks yahi karamchari karta hai.

🌾 Mauja kya hota hai?

🏡 Gaon ka Official Naam:
Mauja aam bhasha mein aapke gaon ke barabar hai — lekin yeh revenue records ka official geographical unit hai. Jamabandi, Khatiyan aur Bhu Naksha — sab kuch Mauja ke naam se hi organize hota hai.

Mauja ke Mukhya Points:

  • Revenue Village: Mauja land records ke liye basic geographical/administrative unit hai.
  • Records ka Base: Jamabandi, Khatiyan, aur Bhu Naksha sabhi Mauja-wise organize hote hain.
  • Panchayat se Alag: Mauja revenue unit hai, Panchayat chunavi (electoral/local governance) unit hai — dono ki boundaries alag ho sakti hain.
  • Har Halka mein Multiple: Ek Halka ke andar kai Mauja aa sakte hain.

💡 Vyavaharik Udaharan (Real Example):

Maan lijiye Ramesh Kumar Patna district ke Phulwari Sharif Anchal mein rehte hain:

District: Patna

Anchal (Circle): Phulwari Sharif

├── Halka: Halka Karamchari ka charge area

└── Mauja: Ramesh ka gaon (jahan unki zameen hai)

🗂️ Bihar Revenue Administration ki Hierarchy

District se lekar aapke gaon tak, revenue records is order mein organize hote hain:

Level Unit Zimmedar Adhikari
1 District (जिला) District Collector / DM
2 Sub-Division (अनुमंडल) Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO)
3 Anchal / Circle (अंचल) Circle Officer (CO)
4 Halka (हल्का) Halka Karamchari (Revenue Karamchari)
5 Mauja (मौजा) Revenue Village — records yahin base hote hain
Visual Hierarchy Tree
District (जिला) │ ├── Sub-Division (अनुमंडल) │ ├── Anchal / Circle (अंचल) │ ├── Halka (हल्का) ──► Halka Karamchari ke charge mein │ └── Mauja (मौजा) ──► Aapka gaon, jahan Jamabandi/Khatiyan record hota hai

💻 Apna Halka aur Mauja Online Kaise Pata Karein?

PORTAL — Bihar Bhumi

Dropdown Selection Method

  1. land.bihar.gov.in/Ror2025/RoR.aspx open karein
  2. Apna District select karein
  3. Anchal (प्रखंड/ब्लॉक) select karein — dropdown mein sabhi Anchal ki list milegi
  4. Halka dropdown se apna Halka select karein
  5. Mauja dropdown se apna gaon (Mauja) select karein
  6. Agar naam pehchan mein na aaye, list mein scroll karke sabhi options dekhein

⚠️ Common Problems aur Solutions

Halka-Mauja select karte waqt aane wali aam samasyaayein aur unka samadhan:

Solution: Kai gaon ka spelling official record mein alag hota hai (jaise local naam se). Poori list scroll karke dekhein ya nearby Mauja names try karein. Confirm karne ke liye Anchal office se poochh sakte hain.
Solution: Ek Anchal mein kai Halka hote hain — agar aapko exact Halka number pata nahi, toh purani Jamabandi rasheed dekhein ya har Halka try karke apna Mauja dhoondhein.
Solution: Yeh normal hai — Mauja revenue unit hai aur Panchayat local governance unit hai, dono ki boundaries match nahi karti. RoR search ke liye hamesha Mauja (revenue village) naam hi use karein, Panchayat naam nahi.
Solution: Apne Anchal (Circle) Office jaakar ya Anchal ki official website/notice board se current Halka Karamchari ka naam aur contact pata kar sakte hain.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Halka Bihar ke revenue administration ki sabse chhoti unit hai, jo ek Halka Karamchari (Revenue Karamchari) ke charge mein hota hai. Ek Halka mein kai Mauja (gaon) shamil hote hain.
Mauja ek revenue village hai — yeh Jamabandi, Khatiyan aur naksha ke liye basic geographical unit hai. Aam bhasha mein Mauja ko gaon ke barabar samjha jaata hai.
Ek District kai Anchal (Circle) mein bata hota hai, har Anchal kai Halka mein, aur har Halka kai Mauja (revenue villages) mein bata hota hai. Yeh Bihar ke revenue administration ka structure hai.
Bihar Bhumi portal (land.bihar.gov.in/Ror2025/RoR.aspx) par District aur Anchal select karne ke baad Halka aur Mauja ki dropdown list dikhti hai — apna gaon dhoondh kar select kar sakte hain.
Halka Karamchari (Revenue Karamchari) ek Halka (kai gaon ka samuh) ke revenue records — jaise Jamabandi, Dakhil Kharij verification aur Lagaan — ka zimmedar sarkari karamchari hota hai.
Mauja ek revenue/land-record unit hai, jabki Panchayat ek local self-governance (chunavi) unit hai. Ek Panchayat mein kai Mauja shamil ho sakte hain, aur dono ki boundaries hamesha match nahi karti.
Purani Jamabandi rasheed, Lagaan rasheed ya registry documents mein Halka aur Mauja ka naam likha hota hai. Nahi mile toh apne local Anchal Office ya Halka Karamchari se sampark karein.
