Chakbandi Kya Hai? Bihar Land Consolidation Guide 2026

💡 Summary Guide 2026

Chakbandi = Bikhri hui zameen ke tukdon ko ek jagah jodna, taaki kheti aasan ho:

📜 Legal Basis: Bihar Consolidation Act, 1956

⚠️ Bihar mein statewide active program nahi hai (2026 tak)

📖 Chakbandi Kya Hota Hai?

📜 Shabdik Arth:
"Chak" ka matlab hai zameen ka tukda, aur "bandi" ka matlab hai vyavastha/ekikaran. Chakbandi yaani ek hi kisan ki gaon mein alag-alag jagah bikhri hui chhoti-chhoti zameen ko unki value ke hisaab se exchange karke, ek ya kam jagahon par judi hui (contiguous) zameen banaana.

Kai kisano ki purkhon se chali aa rahi zameen batwaare ki wajah se gaon mein alag-alag jagah bikhri hoti hai — kabhi 2 katha yahan, kabhi 1 bigha wahan. Chakbandi ka maqsad hai isi zameen ko unki total value barabar rakhte hue, ek jagah par jodkar dena, taaki sinchai, kheti aur dekhbhal aasan ho jaaye.

🗺️ Bihar Mein Chakbandi Ki Current Status

Bihar mein Chakbandi ka kanooni aadhar Bihar Consolidation of Holdings and Prevention of Fragmentation Act, 1956 hai. Ismein 1950-70 ke dashak mein kuch ilaakon mein kaam hua tha, lekin Uttar Pradesh jaise padosi rajya ki tarah Bihar mein yeh ek safal, statewide program nahi ban paaya.

⚠️ Honest Note: Agar aapke gaon mein Chakbandi ki koi active suchana ya notice aaya hai, toh uski sahi jaankari apne local Anchal/Consolidation Officer se hi confirm karein — statewide iski koi ongoing digital tracking system nahi hai.

🛠️ Chakbandi Ka Process (Jab Hota Hai)

  1. 1

    Sarkari Notice

    Gaon mein Chakbandi shuru hone ki sarkari suchana di jaati hai.

  2. 2

    Records Verification

    Consolidation Officer (CO) sabhi zameen records check karke malkiyat confirm karte hain.

  3. 3

    Objections Suni Jaati Hain

    Kisano ki aapattiyan (objections) suni jaati hain aur unka nivaran kiya jaata hai.

  4. 4

    Chak Allotment

    Value ke hisaab se zameen exchange karke har kisan ko ek jagah (chak) mein consolidated zameen di jaati hai.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Chakbandi ek prakriya hai jisme kisan ki bikhri hui zameen ke tukdon ko value ke aadhar par exchange karke ek jagah (contiguous chak) mein jod diya jaata hai.
Kanooni aadhar 1956 se hai, lekin yeh Bihar mein statewide safal program nahi ban paaya. Current status apne local Anchal office se confirm karein.
Special Survey records update/digitize karne ka kaam hai jo Bihar mein abhi active hai. Chakbandi bikhri zameen jodne ka kaam hai jo statewide active nahi hai.
Sarkari notice, records verification, objections suna jaana aur phir value ke hisaab se chak allotment — is tarah process hota hai.
Zameen ek jagah aane se kheti aasan hoti hai, sinchai/transport ki lagat kam hoti hai aur behtar upyog ho paata hai.
